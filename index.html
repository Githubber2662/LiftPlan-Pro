m = entered effective moving-mass estimate (kg); g = 9.81 m/s²; v = rated speed (m/s); k = balance factor; η = assumed efficiency.

Additional design checks

  • Rated motor output and duty cycle
  • Starting and running current
  • Regenerative drive feasibility
  • Brake torque and stopping performance
  • Supply voltage, frequency and phase
  • Short-circuit protection and cable sizing
  • Harmonics, power factor and EMC
  • Standby, lighting and auxiliary loads
Model scope

Energy = estimated power × operating hours × utilization × number of lifts. The estimate excludes standby use, actual duty cycles, regenerative energy, acceleration/load profiles and auxiliary loads.
